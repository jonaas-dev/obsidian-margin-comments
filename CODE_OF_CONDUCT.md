# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Take a correction as information. Give one the same way.
- When someone reports a problem you cannot reproduce, start from the assumption
  that they saw what they say they saw.
- English in issues, pull requests and code, so that everyone can follow.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks; unwanted attention of any kind; publishing
anyone's private information; and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, commits, reviews, discussions —
and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/jonaas-dev). Reports are read, kept to those who need to
act on them, and answered.

The maintainer decides what to do, which may be a warning, removing content, or
blocking an account. Where a decision is about the maintainer, say so in the report and
it goes to GitHub Support instead.
